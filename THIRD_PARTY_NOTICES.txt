Third-party notices for nth-agent
=================================

nth-agent contains code written by others. The licences that code comes under ask that their notices travel
with every copy of it, so they are here: for each piece of it, where it comes from, its version, and its
licence in full. Everything else in nth-agent is its own code, which these licences do not cover.

This list is made from what is linked into each program a release builds
(darwin/arm64, darwin/amd64 and linux/amd64), not kept by hand. The agent's `notices` command prints it.
